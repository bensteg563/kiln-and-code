Questions

What businesses usually want to know.

Do I need to already own a domain?

No. If you already have one it can usually be connected; if not, you can be guided through buying one.

Can you use my existing photos and reviews?

Yes, as long as you have permission to use them. Real work and genuine customer feedback usually make a site much stronger.

Will it work properly on mobile?

Yes. Mobile layout is treated as a core part of the build, not an afterthought.

Can the site be changed later?

Yes. Small ongoing updates can be handled through the optional care plan, or changes can be quoted separately.

Depending on the situation, our lawful basis may include taking steps at your request before a contract, performing a contract, complying with legal obligations, or legitimate interests after considering the impact on the individual.

Our unsolicited email outreach is aimed at corporate businesses and uses verified public business contact routes. We identify Kiln & Code in the message and provide a simple way to object or opt out. We do not treat a public email address as consent, and we do not knowingly send unsolicited electronic-mail marketing to sole traders or other individual subscribers unless a lawful PECR route applies.
